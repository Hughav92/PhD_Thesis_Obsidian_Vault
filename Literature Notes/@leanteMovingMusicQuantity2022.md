> [!Abstract]
>
> This chapter presents an empirical study of North Indian classical music. In particular, it discusses how consideration of the quantity of motion (QoM) extracted through frame differencing from ethnographic footage of concerts can contribute to the understanding of how music is embodied and experienced. In these pages QoM is used to analyze if and how people’s movement varies across a performance and how this variation is related to tempo as well as musical form, including, for example, metrical and cadential structures. The research I discuss here is the first to analyze the QoM of both musicians and listeners across two complete performances in a non-experimental context.
